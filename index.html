01 — Services

What we do, in plain terms.

Hospitality

Short-Term Apartment Rentals

We host travellers in fully furnished apartments — clean linens, fast Wi-Fi, a kitchen that actually works, and someone reachable when something doesn't. Booked through Airbnb, Booking.com and direct enquiry.

  • In-person check-in with key handover
  • Local guidance — restaurants, landmarks
Beauty

Private Manicure Studio

An unhurried, by-appointment-only studio. Classical and gel manicure, nail care. One client at a time, sterilised instruments, hospital-grade hygiene protocol.

  • Booking by appointment — no walk-ins
  • Single-use files and buffers
  • Conservative pricing, transparent menu
Technology

Mobile Applications for Travellers

We design and publish small, focused mobile apps that solve real problems for people driving through Italy. No dark patterns, no ads, no data resold — a one-time purchase and the thing works.

  • ZTL Italia — proximity alerts for 91+ ZTL zones across Italy. Google Play ↗
  • ZTL Navi — turn-by-turn navigation that routes you around restricted-traffic zones. In development.
  • Privacy-first — location stays on the device
02 — About

A small company, run by hand.

2Alfa d.o.o. was founded in Slovenia in 2018. We are a single-shareholder private limited company — not a marketplace, not a chain, not a venture. Every apartment is hosted personally, every appointment is booked personally, and every line of code in our apps is reviewed before release.

We believe in finishing what we start, replying when someone writes, and keeping prices honest. Boring values; they tend to age well.
