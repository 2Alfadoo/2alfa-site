About the company

A Slovenian company
built on doing the work.

2Alfa d.o.o. is a privately held Slovenian company — small, focused, and deliberately so. We operate in three fields, each by hand, each with the same standard.

The story, briefly.

The company was founded in Slovenia in 2018 by a single shareholder, with a simple idea: rather than spreading thin across markets, do a few real things well. Apartments that you would yourself want to stay in. A manicure studio that respects your time and your nails. Mobile apps that do exactly what they say, charge once, and never resell your data.

Most companies our size try to look bigger. We try to look honest. The owner answers the email. The owner reviews the code. The owner walks through the apartment before the next guest. There is no marketing department because there is no need for one.

What we believe.

Finish what you start. Every apartment listing, every app version, every appointment is treated as something we'll have to defend tomorrow. So we don't ship anything we wouldn't want to defend.

Charge fairly. Our apartment rates are not the cheapest and not the highest — they reflect the actual cost of running a clean, well-equipped place with someone reachable. Our apps are one-time purchases under €10, with no subscriptions and no upsells. The salon menu is fixed and visible.

Keep data on the device. Our apps don't have servers collecting your location. The booking confirmations from our apartments are sent through your booking platform, not stored by us. The salon keeps a paper appointment book.

Reply. Within one business day, in five languages.
